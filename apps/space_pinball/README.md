# Space Pinball

Lean your badge to roll the ball around a space pinball table inspired by the classic Windows 3D Pinball: Space Cadet.

## Usage

Hold the badge upright and press SELECT to start. Your badge's motion sensor
turns the table into a real tilt table: lean left or right and the ball rolls
that way. Give the badge a quick flick to bump the table, but flick too often
and you'll get a DANGER warning and then TILT, which locks your flippers until
the ball drains.

| Button | Action |
| --- | --- |
| LEFT / RIGHT | Left and right flippers |
| SELECT (or DOWN) | Hold to pull the plunger, let go to launch |
| UP | Bump the table (counts toward TILT) |
| MENU | Level the table at the angle you're holding it |
| BACK | Pause |
| HOME | Return to the menu |

Complete missions to climb from CADET to FLEET ADMIRAL:

- **Target Practice:** knock down the three drop targets on the left wall.
- **Launch Training:** hit the pop bumpers eight times.
- **Re-Entry:** light the three lanes at the top.
- **Hyperspace:** send the ball into the wormhole.

Light all three top lanes to raise the bonus multiplier, up to x5. Knock down
all the drop targets to relight the kickback in the left outlane. You get three
balls per game, plus a ball save for the first six seconds of each launch. Your
high score is saved on the badge.

The game needs no Wi-Fi or extra hardware. All artwork is original and drawn
in code; no graphics or sounds come from the original game.

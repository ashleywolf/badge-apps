# Ski Tilt

Ski Tilt is a SkiFree-style downhill game where you tilt the badge to steer and lean your skier.

## How to play

Tilt the badge left or right. Your skier leans with the badge and carves
across the slope. Point straight down to go fastest. Turn across the hill to
slow down.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tilt left / right | Steer and lean the skier |
| LEFT / RIGHT | Steer without tilting |
| DOWN (hold) | Tuck for extra speed |
| UP (hold) | Dig in and stop |
| SELECT | Start, hop, or spin while in the air |
| BACK | Pause or resume |
| MENU | Flip the tilt direction (saved) |

Dodge trees, rocks, and stumps. Moguls slow you down and bounce you a little.
Yellow ramps launch you into the air. Press SELECT in the air to spin for
+100 style, but finish the spin before you land or you wipe out.

Past 2000 m, a yeti comes down the mountain after you. A red **!** on the
edge of the screen shows where it is. Tuck to stay ahead for as long as you
can. Your best distance is saved.

## Requirements

Ski Tilt uses the badge's built-in accelerometer and needs no Wi-Fi or
`secrets.py`. If the accelerometer reads nothing, use LEFT and RIGHT to steer.
If tilting steers the wrong way on your badge, press MENU once.

All art is drawn in code for this app. Ski Tilt is a fan tribute to the
classic SkiFree and is not affiliated with it.
